Terms of Use

These terms describe the intended use of the MediQueue application.

Authorized use

MediQueue is intended for authorized hospital, clinic and healthcare operations. Users must use accounts only for legitimate responsibilities assigned to them.

Account responsibility

Users are responsible for protecting their credentials and for the accuracy of information they enter or update.

Clinical responsibility

MediQueue supports workflow and record management; it does not replace professional medical judgment. Clinical decisions remain the responsibility of qualified healthcare professionals.

Availability

For production use, organizations should maintain appropriate backups, security controls, availability procedures and compliance processes for their environment.
