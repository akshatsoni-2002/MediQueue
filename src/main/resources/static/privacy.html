Privacy Policy

This page explains the privacy principles used for the MediQueue application.

Information we handle

MediQueue may handle account information, patient and appointment details, queue status and operational records required to provide the hospital workflow.

Purpose

Information is used to authenticate authorized users, manage appointments and queues, support clinical workflow and deliver relevant notifications.

Access

Access is controlled by user roles such as Doctor, Staff and Admin. Users should only access information necessary for their assigned responsibilities.

Security

MediQueue is designed with authenticated access, role-based authorization and controlled operational workflows. Production deployments should also use HTTPS, secure credentials, backups and appropriate organizational privacy controls.
